SOC LAB: SUSPICIOUS LOGIN INVESTIGATION

Data: Synthetic training logs.
No real accounts were accessed or changed.

Detection rule:
Five or more failed logins followed by a successful login
for the same user and source IP within five minutes.

Results:
15 records analysed.
Two alerts generated: charlie and diana.

CHARLIE — INITIAL EVIDENCE
Source IP: 198.51.100.50
Failed attempts: 5
First failure: 2026-10-05 10:00:00 UTC
Successful login: 2026-10-05 10:02:30 UTC

Initial assessment:
Suspicious login sequence. These logs alone cannot confirm
whether the account was compromised.

HYPOTHETICAL FOLLOW-UP
For this exercise, assume Charlie denies the login and
the device is unfamiliar. These details are scenario
assumptions, not evidence contained in the CSV.

Assessment:
Suspected account compromise requiring escalation.

Proposed response:
1. Preserve authentication and related activity logs.
2. Review MFA results, device details and post-login activity.
3. Escalate to the senior SOC analyst.
4. Follow the approved playbook to revoke sessions and
   reset the password; consider temporary account disablement.
5. Document findings and monitor for further suspicious activity.

Limitations:
No MFA, device or post-login evidence was provided.
No containment actions were performed.
Diana's alert has not yet been investigated.

DIANA — TRAINING INVESTIGATION

CSV evidence:
Five failed logins followed by a successful login from
203.0.113.25 between 11:00:00 and 11:02:30 UTC.

Hypothetical follow-up findings:
Diana confirms repeated password typing errors.
The device matches her usual device.
MFA completed successfully.
No unusual post-login activity was identified.

These follow-up findings are fictional scenario details,
not evidence contained in the CSV.

Assessment:
Likely benign login activity. The rule detected the intended
pattern, but the additional context supports a false positive
for malicious activity.

Proposed disposition:
Document the supporting evidence and close as benign under
the team's review procedure. No containment is proposed
based on the supplied scenario.

Lesson:
The same login pattern can have different explanations.
An alert requires investigation before a final decision.
